## Common SEO Questions

### Q: When will the site rank in Google?
**A**: Usually 2-4 weeks after sitemap submission. Initial rankings may be lower, gradually improving with more organic traffic and backlinks.

### Q: How often should I update the sitemap?
**A**: When you add significant content (new products/projects). The current sitemap covers main sections and updates automatically.

### Q: Do I need to pay for better rankings?
**A**: No. This is pure organic SEO. The website is optimized for natural, free search traffic. You may optionally use Google Ads for faster traffic.

### Q: What about backlinks?
**A**: Quality over quantity. Get links from industry sites (stone suppliers, architecture sites, home improvement blogs). Avoid link schemes.

### Q: How long does SEO take to show results?
**A**: 3-6 months for significant results. Some pages may rank within weeks, others take longer. Patience is key.
